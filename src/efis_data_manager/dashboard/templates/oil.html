Oil Consumption

Track oil consumption over time. Log each top-off and oil change below; the tool computes consumption rate (quarts per engine hour) between events and a rolling average. Consumption is measured from what you replenish, so accurate tach readings and quart amounts matter. Oil events are stored in the Analysis tool, separate from the flight data imported by the Data Manager.

Consumption Rate ?Consumption rate (qt/hr) vs engine tach time. Orange dots/line are per-period rate; the blue line is the rolling average over your Trend Window. Vertical red lines mark oil changes. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
